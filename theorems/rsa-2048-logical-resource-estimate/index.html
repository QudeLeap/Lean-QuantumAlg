Logical-resource estimate for breaking RSA-2048

Theorem statement

Suppose $(p,q)$ is drawn uniformly from the ordered pairs of distinct primes satisfying $2^{1023}<p,q<2^{1024}$, with known $N=pq$ satisfying $2^{2047}<N<2^{2048}$. There exists a quantum algorithm that returns a factor $d\in\{p,q\}$ with probability at least $0.99$, using at most $4097$ logical qubits, $1.18\times10^{17}$ Toffoli gates, $4.17\times10^{14}$ CNOT gates, $1.49\times10^8$ single-qubit gates, $1.18\times10^{17}$ quantum computation time, $132$ GB of classical memory, and $1.54\times10^{19}$ classical operations.
